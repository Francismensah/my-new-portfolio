About

Reliability engineer.
Infrastructure thinker.

I'm a Site Reliability Engineer focused on designing, operating, and continuously improving the reliability of cloud-native production systems in high-transaction environments.

I have owned the reliability posture of mission-critical services across consumer products, internal platforms, and high-transaction workloads. I operationalize reliability through well-defined SLIs, SLOs, and error budgets.
