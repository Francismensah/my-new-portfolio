About

Reliability engineer.
Infrastructure thinker.

I own the reliability posture of mission-critical production systems across customer-facing and internal platforms. I operationalize reliability through well-defined SLIs, SLOs, and error budgets to ensure availability, latency, and performance targets are consistently met while enabling rapid product delivery.

My work spans the full reliability lifecycle, from infrastructure design to incident response and continuous optimization.

Core areas of expertise:

  • Designing and managing scalable AWS infrastructure using Terraform (Infrastructure as Code)
  • Building resilient CI/CD pipelines that improve deployment safety and release velocity
  • Designing observability and incident telemetry to improve MTTD, reduce MTTR, and strengthen alert quality across production systems
  • Leading incident response, root cause analysis, and blameless postmortems
  • Reducing operational toil through automation, standardization, and system hardening
  • Architecting Kubernetes-based workloads for resilience and scalability

I treat reliability as a product, balancing stability, cost efficiency, and engineering velocity while driving operational maturity across systems.

Passionate about building production platforms that scale predictably, fail gracefully, and recover quickly.
